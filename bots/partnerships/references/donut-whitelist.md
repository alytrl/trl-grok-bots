## Commercial defs (Alyssa, 2026-09-06)
- **Currency:** USD. **Billing:** Donut's team handles package billing; TRL/Alyssa does not invoice those fees.
- **Asset:** essentially one ad (static or video unit toward the tier asset range).
- **Iteration:** a revision or variation of that ad (counts against iteration allowance).
- **30-day paid usage:** starts when the media buyer actually launches the ad — not at creative delivery.
- Ad spend / creator fees: do not assume in or out of package price unless the live sheet or Alyssa says so.

## Product split
- **Whitelist Creative** — static/video packages + Joaquin/Aira routing
- **Advertorial** — editorial/lander pages (Curativa Bay pattern)
- **Donut Outreach** — newsletter sponsor sales (separate)
